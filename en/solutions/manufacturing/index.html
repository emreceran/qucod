ERP for Manufacturers

From work order to shipment, from materials to installation, we track all your production in one system. Proven Odoo solutions for companies that manufacture by project.

The most expensive thing in production is not being able to see. Which job is where, which material is missing, when the shipment goes — if these are scattered across paper and Excel, delays and costs get out of control.

We model your production in Odoo by project: work orders, bill of materials (BOM), production stages, shipping and installation in one flow. Mobile data from the shop floor, and a live production dashboard for management. We have implemented this end to end at precast and other manufacturing companies.

FAQ

About manufacturing ERP

We manufacture by project (make-to-order). Is it suitable?

Yes, that is exactly our speciality. We set it up so that you can plan and cost each job or project separately.

How will we enter data from the shop floor?

Work order status and material movements are entered from the site or shop floor with a mobile app or a terminal, and posted to the ERP immediately.

Which manufacturing sectors do you have experience in?

We have carried out end-to-end implementations in project-based manufacturing, above all precast buildings and door production.

Will it work with our existing machine/data systems?

We design integrations around your processes; we migrate existing data and provide the connections needed.
