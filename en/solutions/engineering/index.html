ERP for Engineering & Contracting

We bring sales, purchasing, expenses, timesheets and progress billing for your projects into one system and make real project profitability visible. Proven in project-based companies.

In contracting, the answer to "did we make a profit?" usually comes once the job is finished, and even then as an estimate. If expenses, materials, labour and progress billing are in different places, project profitability is never clearly visible.

We set up every job in Odoo as a project: sales, purchasing, expenses, timesheets and progress billing are linked to the same project. Management sees the project's real cost and profit live.
