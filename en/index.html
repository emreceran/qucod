Q · C · D

Our name comes from three words

Qucod stands for Quality · Cost · Delivery, the three core measures of a factory in lean manufacturing. Every system we build exists to make these three visible and manageable.

Q

Quality

Catch defects at the source

Quality checks sit inside production. Which batch, which machine, which shift: wherever a defect appears, it is recorded there.

  • Quality control steps and sign-offs
  • Defect and scrap records with causes
  • Lot and serial traceability
  • Warranty and service history
C

Cost

Know which work makes money

Material, labour and overhead are written to every work order. The cost you quoted sits next to the cost you actually incurred.

  • Product and work order cost
  • Quoted vs actual comparison
  • Project and order profitability
  • Cash tied up in stock, made visible
D

Delivery

Keep the date you promised

Capacity, material and sequence are planned together. Lead times come from the real load on your machines, not a guess.

  • Production plan and capacity
  • Material requirements and purchasing
  • Lead-time calculation and delay alerts
  • Shipping and installation tracking

FAQ

Common questions

What does Qucod mean?

Quality, Cost, Delivery. In lean manufacturing, a factory's performance is judged by these three measures. The systems we build exist to make all three measurable and manageable every day.

Which software do you use?

We implement a system based on open-source Odoo Community. There is no license fee. The parts specific to your industry and business, we develop ourselves.

Do we have to replace our accounting software?

No. We connect to your current accounting package and transfer records to it. If you want, accounting can move into the same system over time.

Does our data go to an AI?

Only if you allow it, and only as much as you allow. The reporting connection is read-only, and you choose between full data, masked data or no sharing at all. The system works fully without AI.

How long does it take?

It depends on scope. The exact timeline and price are given in writing at the end of the Design stage. Because go-live happens module by module, you see the first results early.

We already have an ERP. What happens to it?

Your data is migrated to the new system. Both can run side by side for a while during the transition; we set the go-live date together.
