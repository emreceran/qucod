AI reporting

From the granary of data to the answer you need.

An ambar is a granary: where a year's harvest is gathered and kept. Your company's granary is its data. AI reporting gathers all of it and sifts it down to the question you ask.

  • No hunting through report menus. You type the question.
  • The connection is read-only: the AI cannot change or delete records.
  • You decide what is shared: everything, a masked version, or nothing.

FAQ

Common questions

Which software do you use?

We implement a system based on open-source Odoo Community. There is no license fee. The parts specific to your industry and business, we develop ourselves.

Do we have to replace our accounting software?

No. We connect to your current accounting package and transfer records to it. If you want, accounting can move into the same system over time.

Does our data go to an AI?

Only if you allow it, and only as much as you allow. The reporting connection is read-only, and you choose between full data, masked data or no sharing at all. The system works fully without AI.

How long does it take?

It depends on scope. The exact timeline and price are given in writing at the end of the Design stage. Because go-live happens module by module, you see the first results early.

Where does the system run?

On your own server or on a cloud server of your choice. Backups and updates are part of the implementation plan.

We already have an ERP. What happens to it?

Your data is migrated to the new system. Both can run side by side for a while during the transition; we set the go-live date together.
