Field Operations

What the disconnect between field and office costs your company

July 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Installation, service, commissioning or a construction site — the field is where the work happens. But the office is where decisions are made. The slower the bridge of information between the two, the later and the more wrongly the company decides.

What does the disconnect look like?

In most companies, field information reaches the office like this: photos sent over WhatsApp in the evening, envelopes of receipts collected at the weekend, working hours that people try to remember at month end. The result:

  • Expenses are late and incomplete. The receipt for a purchase in the field gets lost or is entered days later; the real cost is never clear.
  • Hours are worked out by guesswork. Who worked how many hours on which project is not known for certain — both payroll and project cost are estimates.
  • The status of the work is unclear. Is the task done, half done, waiting? The office only finds out by calling to ask.
  • Decisions are delayed. The problem arises in the field, reaches the office days later, and the solution goes back later still.
The later information reaches the office from the field, the less it is worth. Learning today about a problem that happened yesterday usually means learning too late.

What does a real-time flow of data change?

An app in the field team's pocket cuts this bridge down to seconds:

Tasks are managed from the field

The employee sees the job assigned to them and updates its status ("started / done / waiting") on the spot. The office knows where the job stands without asking.

Expenses are entered on the spot

The moment money is spent, it goes into the system with a photo of the receipt. No more lost receipts or forgotten expenses; project cost is in real time.

Hours and timesheets are kept accurately

Who worked how long on which project — entered from the field, flowing as accurate data into both payroll and project profitability.

Location and timestamp

With an on-site check-in, who started where and when is on record. It works offline and synchronises automatically when the connection returns.
