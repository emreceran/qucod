Odoo Mobile App Development

We develop mobile apps that connect your team in the field to your Odoo ERP in real time. Orders, inventory, customers and tasks — all in a single source of data, in an app that works even offline.

Odoo's web interface is strong on the desktop; but the real work happens in the field. The sales rep is with the customer, the storekeeper is at the shelf, the technician is on a service call. If phones, WhatsApp and paper slips sit in between, data is being entered late, incompletely or wrongly. That disconnect costs time and money every day.

We close this gap with mobile apps that work fully integrated with your Odoo ERP. The app talks to Odoo's official API (JSON-RPC); it is not a separate system but the extension of your ERP in the field. Every transaction entered in the field is posted immediately to the Odoo database, the single source of data.

How It Works

The extension of your ERP in the field

Not a separate island; an app that connects to your existing Odoo installation and works as one with it.

01

Connect

The app connects securely to the Odoo API (JSON-RPC); users log in with their own Odoo permissions.

02

Work in the field

Orders, stock, visits and tasks are entered in the field — if offline, they are held on the device.

03

Sync

When the connection returns, all transactions are written to the Odoo database; no duplicate records, no gaps.

04

A single truth

The office sees accurate data in real time; reports in Odoo stay up to date.

FAQ

About Odoo mobile apps

Does the Odoo mobile app use a separate database?

No. The app talks directly to Odoo's official API (JSON-RPC); the single source of data is your Odoo database. Orders, stock and customer transactions entered in the field are posted to the ERP immediately.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. We design it offline-first; the field team can work where there is no connection, and the data synchronises with Odoo automatically when the connection returns.

Which technology do you build with?

React Native (Expo), giving both Android and iOS from a single codebase; Odoo 18 Community in the background, with modules specific to your business where needed.

Will it integrate with our existing Odoo installation?

Yes. It connects to your existing Odoo Community or Enterprise installation; where needed, we add modules specific to your processes and shape the app around your business.
