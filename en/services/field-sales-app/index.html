Field Sales Mobile App

We bring your sales reps and field sales team together in one mobile app: customer visits, instant orders, van-sales invoicing and payment collection — all in real time in your Odoo.

In field sales time is everything; but paper orders and entering data afterwards kill that time. If the rep has no current stock and prices to hand while with the customer, and the order is written on paper and entered back at the office, then errors, delays and lost orders are inevitable.

Our field sales app gives the rep customer, stock and price information instantly; it creates the order on the spot, issues an invoice or delivery note from the vehicle if needed and records the payment. All transactions reach Odoo in real time.

FAQ

About the field sales app

I don't use Odoo. Can it still work?

You get the best results with Odoo, because it gives you a single source of data. If you do not have Odoo, we can implement that too.

Does it work in areas with no internet?

Yes, we design it to work offline; the data synchronises automatically when the connection returns.

Can I issue invoices and delivery notes from the vehicle?

Yes; we set up the invoice/delivery note and payment collection flow in the vehicle to match your e-document processes.

Which devices does it run on?

Built with React Native, it runs on both Android and iOS phones and tablets.
