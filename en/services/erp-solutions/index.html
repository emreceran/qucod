Odoo ERP

ERP Solutions for Your Business

We take sales, inventory, purchasing, production and accounting out of scattered files and bring them together in one system. With Odoo, in phases and with no licence fees.

As you grow, Excel and separate programs stop being enough. Sales is in one place, inventory in another, accounting somewhere else entirely; you enter the same data more than once, the "which one is right?" problem grows and decisions come late.

An ERP brings all these processes together in one system. With Odoo we set this up in a way that suits small and mid-sized businesses, step by step: we get the most critical area working first, then extend. Thanks to the Community edition there are no licence fees.

FAQ

About ERP implementation

Which ERP do you implement?

Odoo. Thanks to its modular structure and the open-source Community edition, it is the most flexible and cost-effective option for small and mid-sized businesses.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on the scope; we work in phases. The first critical modules usually go live within a few weeks.

Will my existing data be migrated?

Yes. We clean up and migrate data such as customers and suppliers, products, stock and open balances.

Is accounting included?

Day-to-day bookkeeping is managed in Odoo; for statutory accounting we can integrate with the accounting software you already use.
