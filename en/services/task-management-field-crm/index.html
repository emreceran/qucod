Field teams

Task Management & Field CRM

We manage the work of your field and service teams in one app: task assignment, status tracking, service forms, photos and digital signatures — all connected to your Odoo.

If the field team is disorganised, so is the work. Who is where, which job is finished, what the customer was told — when these get lost in phone calls and WhatsApp, follow-up becomes impossible.

Our Tasks & CRM app assigns, tracks and documents the work. The technician fills in the form on site and adds photos and a signature; management sees the live status in Odoo.

Capabilities

What does the field team manage?

The whole flow, from task assignment to proof of service.

Task assignment & tracking

Assign tasks, prioritise them and follow their status in real time.

Check-in & location

Start and end of each visit or service call, with location.

Service form

Customer record

Yes, digital signatures and photo attachments are standard features.
